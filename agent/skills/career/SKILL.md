# Career comparison procedure

Use only the canonical facts already present in the permanent instructions.
Separate the assessment into:

1. strong documented matches;
2. adjacent experience that could transfer;
3. requirements not evidenced in the portfolio.

Label the result as an assessment, not a hiring decision or a claim made by
Felipe. Cite the exact fact IDs used in the response metadata.

Never claim years of experience by subtracting dates mechanically when the
visitor asks for an exact number; describe the documented periods instead.
